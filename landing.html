Everything, in one place

Most trackers do one thing. Daylog brings your habits, time, health and thoughts together — and lets you hide anything you don't use.

⏱️

24-hour time tracking

Tap an activity to start, tap another to switch. See exactly where your day goes on a colour-coded timeline.

✅

Habits & streaks

A daily checklist with fire-streaks and 90-day heatmaps. Build the chain, keep it alive.

📷

Photos & voice notes

Add a photo of your day or record a voice note while it is fresh. They stay on your phone and go in your backup file.

🍅

Focus & timeboxing

A Pomodoro timer, a day planner and binaural beats for focus or sleep — with alarms that ring even when the app is closed.

💪

Gym companion

A full workout split with animated form demos, per-set logging and muscle-group stats.

📊

Stats that mean something

Trends, auto-insights, a weekly review and a Polymath Index across body, mind, work & discipline.

📆

Calendar & reminders

A month tinted by your mood, dated events, and full-screen alarms that wake your phone.

✍️

Journal & write

Voice-to-text journaling plus a block editor for longer notes, checklists and articles.

🎨

Make it yours

Rename, recolour, reorder or hide every habit, field, workout, tab and theme. It adapts to you.
