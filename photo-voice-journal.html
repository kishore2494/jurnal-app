Quick answer

Daylog is a free journal app for Android and the web where each day can hold photos and recorded voice notes alongside a written entry, mood and habits. Photos are resized on the phone and their location data is removed. Everything is stored on the device and included in your backup file.

Why keep pictures and voice in your journal

Some days are easier to say than to type, and some are best remembered as a picture. Daylog puts both on the same day as your mood and habits, so looking back shows the whole day, and “On this day” brings them back a year later.

Common questions

Where are my photos and voice notes stored?

On your phone, in Daylog’s own storage. They are not uploaded to any server, and they are not sent to the optional Google Sheet sync.

Will they fill up my phone?

Photos are resized on the phone to a few hundred kilobytes each, and voice notes use about 4 KB per second. Settings ▸ Backup & restore shows how much space they take.

What happens if I change phones?

Export a backup file from Settings ▸ Backup & restore and import it on the new phone. The backup includes your photos and voice notes. Because there is no cloud copy, uninstalling without a backup deletes your data.

Can I share a photo?

Yes. Open it full-screen and tap Share. There is also a share card with up to nine of a month’s photos.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads and nothing paywalled. Optional tips through Google Play unlock nothing.
