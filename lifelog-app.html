Quick answer

Daylog is a free lifelog app for Android and the web. Each day holds your habits, mood and energy, a journal entry, photos and voice notes, automatic screen time, and a one-tap 24-hour time log. Everything stays on your phone, and the patterns are worked out on the device.

What goes into one day

Most journaling apps keep words and most trackers keep numbers. A lifelog is both, and Daylog keeps them on the same day so you can look back and see the whole picture, not fragments spread across five apps.

📷
Photos and voice notes

Add a photo from the camera or gallery and record a voice note. They are kept on the phone and saved in your backup file.

😊
Mood, energy and a journal

One tap on the mood grid sets both, then write a few lines, by typing or with voice typing, and tag them with #topics.

✅
Habits with streaks

A daily checklist with streaks, skip days for rest and a strength score, so one missed day does not erase months.

📱
Screen time and Health Connect

Screen time is measured automatically, and steps and sleep come from Health Connect, each with its own switch.

⏱️
A 24-hour time log

Tap what you are doing when you start; the day becomes a colour-coded timeline.

🔁
On this day

The Log shows your entry and photos from a week, a month and a year ago.

Common questions

What is a lifelog app?

A lifelog app keeps a record of your days: what you did, how you felt and what happened. Daylog combines a habit tracker, a mood journal, photos, voice notes, screen time and a time log in one daily record.

Does Daylog need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Open it and start logging.

Is my data private?

Entries, photos and voice notes are stored on your phone. Daylog has no account and no server of its own. The one optional exception is a sync to a Google Sheet that you own, and photos and voice notes are never sent there.

What happens if I change phones?

Export a backup file from Settings ▸ Backup & restore and import it on the new phone. The backup includes your photos and voice notes. Because there is no cloud copy, uninstalling without a backup deletes your data.

Is it free?

Yes. Nothing is locked behind a paywall and there are no ads. Optional tips through Google Play unlock nothing.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. On an iPhone you can use the web version and add it to your home screen.
