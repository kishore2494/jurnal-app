How to use Daylog

A private, offline tracker for your whole life. Here's a 2-minute tour of everything it does — nothing is uploaded anywhere, it all lives on your phone.

1

Log your day

Your daily 60-second check-in.

  • Rate your mood and energy 1–10, jot sleep and deep-work hours.
  • Tick the habits you did — each builds a streak.
  • Add a quick reflection and journal — tap 🎤 to speak instead of type.
  • Scroll down for the optional Deep log — richer metrics when you want them.
👉 Try: tap a few habits, set your mood, and hit Save. That's a logged day.
2

Track your time ⭐

The signature feature — see where your 24 hours actually go.

  • Tap an activity (Work, Travel, Eating…) to start its timer.
  • Tap another to switch instantly — the old one stops itself.
  • Your day fills in as a colored 24-hour timeline you can tap to fix.
  • Forgot to track? Add a block by hand any time.
👉 Try: tap Travel when you leave home, Work when you arrive — watch the timeline build.
3

Habits & streaks

Consistency at a glance.

  • Every habit shows a 🔥 streak and a 90-day heatmap.
  • Green squares are days you did it — keep the chain unbroken.
  • The percentage shows your last-30-day consistency.
4

Gym

A full workout companion.

  • A 6-day split — tap a day to see its exercises.
  • Each move has an animated demo, form tip, and a spot to log reps/weight.
  • Tick exercises off; your gym streak and volume feed into Stats.
👉 Try: don't lift? Hide the whole Gym tab in Customize.
5

Calendar & alarms

Your month, colored by how you felt.

  • Each day is tinted by your mood, with dots for gym, tracked time and events.
  • Add events on any date with a loud alarm — even full-screen over your lock screen.
  • Tap a day to see its summary or jump into its log.
6

Write

Your inner blog — journals, notes, plans in long form.

  • Build articles from blocks: headings, text, checklists, bullets.
  • Tap 🎤 to dictate straight into any block.
  • Drag to reorder; everything saves as you type.
7

Stats & insights

What all that tracking adds up to.

  • Trends for mood, energy, sleep and time — pick 7 / 14 / 30 / 90 days.
  • Auto insights like "commute costs you 2h/day" and sleep-vs-mood links.
  • A weekly review and a Polymath Index scoring body, mind, work, learning & discipline.
8

Make it yours

Nothing here is fixed. In More ▸ Customize:

  • Add, rename, recolor, hide or reorder every habit, activity, field and workout.
  • Rearrange or hide the bottom tabs; pick a theme colour.
  • Edit the deep-log sections and the gym split to match your life.
👉 Try: hide anything you don't use — the app shrinks to just what matters to you.
9

Your data & privacy

Private by design — and that has one flip side.

  • Everything lives only on this phone. No account, no cloud, no tracking.
  • So export a backup now and then (More ▸ Your data) — uninstalling erases everything.
  • Set daily reminders with notification or full-screen alarm modes.
10

Your patterns — real insights 🧠

Daylog mines your raw data on your phone and tells you what actually works for you.

  • Your sleep sweet spot — which sleep range gives you the best mood.
  • Which habit actually lifts your mood, with honest sample sizes.
  • Your natural bed → wake time and peak focus window from tracked time.
  • What heavy screen days cost you, your mood trend, and more.
👉 All of it is computed on your device — nothing is uploaded, ever.
11

Auto-tracking 📱

Data that collects itself.

  • Screen time syncs automatically from your phone — charted daily and correlated with your mood.
  • Focus sessions, tasks completed and words written are counted for you.
  • Steps, sleep & calories via Health Connect are coming next.
  • Every signal has an on/off switch in Settings ▸ Auto-tracking — off means not collected.
12

Search & the Menu 🔍

Everything else is one tap away.

  • Search finds anything you ever wrote — journals, tasks, notes, plans, articles. Try a #tag.
  • Pin any tabs you like to the bottom bar — ☰ Menu ▸ Edit tabs.
  • Voice typing: tap 🎤 Speak on any journal or note and talk.
  • New here? Take the guided tour: Settings ▸ Take the app tour.
13

The bits that pay you back 🎯

Three things at the top of your Log that make it worth opening.

  • Today ring — one dial showing how much of today you've filled in. Every field and habit you tick closes it a little; it buzzes and turns green when the day is complete.
  • On this day — your own entry from a week, a month, three months or a year ago, right above today's. Tap it to open that day. Your old entries finally earn their keep.
  • Mood grid — instead of two sliders, tap one square to set your mood and your energy at once (pleasant ↔ unpleasant across, high ↔ low energy up and down). The grid then offers ten precise words for that feeling — tap one to tag your entry with it, tap again to remove. Those tags are searchable later.
👉 Try: tap the top-right square on the mood grid, then tap Energised. Now search #energised in a month's time.
14

Counted habits & skip days 🔢

Not every habit is a yes/no, and not every missed day is a failure.

  • Give a habit a daily goal — "at least 8 glasses of water", "at least 20 pages", or a cut-down goal like "at most 2 coffees". Set them in Customize ▸ Checklist habits and tap the 🎯 button on any habit.
  • A counted habit becomes a tap counter: tap the chip to add one, − to undo. "At most" habits also get a 0 button to log a clean zero, and turn red if you go over.
  • Skip a day without losing your streak — tap a habit a second time to mark it skipped. Rest days, sick days and holidays stop counting as failures, and they're left out of your completion percentage too.
  • Habit strength — every habit gets a score out of 100 on the Habits screen. It's a weighted trend, so one missed day can't wipe out months of work and one good day can't fake it.
  • Habit ideas — tap ✨ Browse habit ideas under your checklist for a gallery of starters across Health, Mind, Productivity and Cut down.
👉 Try: Browse habit ideas ▸ add Water, then tap the chip eight times.
15

Reminders that actually ring ⏰

Set a time and Daylog takes over your screen — even locked.

  • Add reminders in Settings ▸ Reminders. Each one can be a full-screen alarm (loud, over the lock screen, with Dismiss and Snooze 5 min) or just a quiet notification — tap the mode to switch.
  • Android needs your permission for on-the-minute alarms. On Android 14 and newer, Android blocks exact alarms by default. Daylog asks once, and you can allow it any time from the warning in Settings ▸ Reminders. Without it your reminder still fires, but Android decides when — up to several minutes late.
  • Your alarms survive a restart — they're restored automatically when your phone boots.
  • If your phone blocks the full-screen alarm, Daylog still rings loudly as a notification so you never silently miss one.
  • Choose your own alarm tone — Customize ▸ Alarm sound. Pick any alarm sound already on your phone (or one of your own audio files), preview it before committing, and switch vibration on or off. The same tone is used whether the full-screen alarm opens or it rings as a notification.
  • On Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO, realme, OPPO, vivo and OnePlus phones, also switch on Autostart and set battery usage to No restrictions — those systems stop alarms otherwise.
👉 Try: Settings ▸ Reminders ▸ ⏰ Test full-screen alarm (1 min), then lock your phone and wait.
16

Your year in pixels 🗓️

A whole year of your life as one picture.

  • At the bottom of Stats, every day of the year is one small coloured square — red through green, from the mood you logged.
  • Rows are months, columns are days, so patterns jump out: a bad fortnight, a good summer, the week you stopped logging.
  • Tap any square to open that day's entry.
17

Journal prompts 📝

A blank box is the hardest thing to write in.

  • Above the journal box, tap 🙏 Gratitude, 🌙 Brain dump, ⭐ Highlights or 💡 Idea to drop in a ready-made prompt.
  • Use #tags anywhere — they link entries together and are searchable.
  • Prefer talking? Tap 🎤 Speak and dictate.
18

Make the Log yours 🧩

If you don't use something, hide it. If you use it most, put it first.

  • Customize ▸ Log screen sections — drag to reorder every card on your Log (Today ring, On this day, mood grid, Tasks, checklist, Workout, Health, Reflection, Deep log, Sunday review) and tap 👁 to hide any of them.
  • Customize ▸ Log screen fields — rename or hide individual fields, including the reflection questions.
  • Settings ▸ Log screen widgets — quick on/off switches for the ring, On this day, the mood grid and the haptic buzz.
  • Theme — Light, Dark navy, Black, or Auto to follow your phone.
  • Pin whichever tabs you want to the bottom bar: ☰ Menu ▸ Edit tabs.
19

Focus, sounds & the gym 🎧

The tools around the tracking.

  • Focus — a Pomodoro timer with an ongoing notification, plus timeboxing for planning a block of work.
  • Waves — binaural focus tones you can play while you work.
  • Gym — an editable split, animated exercise demos, per-set logging and muscle-group breakdowns. Reachable from the Log or ☰ Menu.
  • Next-day mood effects — Stats tells you how a habit today changes your mood tomorrow, with a confidence rating. A next-day effect can't be explained away by your mood causing the habit.
